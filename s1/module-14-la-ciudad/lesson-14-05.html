📋 THE WEEK ON ONE PAGE

Here's everything Module 14 gave you — keep this open while you practice:

Say this……when you mean
… está a la derecha / a la izquierda… is on the right / on the left
sigue derechogo / keep straight ahead
dobla a la derecha / a la izquierdaturn right / turn left
… está cerca de / lejos de…… is near / far from …
Voy a la plaza. / Vamos al parque.I'm going to the plaza. / We're going to the park.
la calle, la avenida, la esquina, la cuadrathe street, the avenue, the corner, the block

The whole engine is just two verbs: estar for where a place is, ir a for where you're going — with the fixed direction phrases dropped in between.
