LESSON 7.02 · THE PATTERN
The stem e becomes i — everywhere but nosotros
Same boot shape you already know. Only the change itself is new: this time e turns into i.
👋 THE ONE IDEA
In an e→i boot verb, the stem vowel e changes to i in every present form — except nosotros, which keeps the original e.

You already know the endings for regular -IR verbs. A boot verb keeps those same endings; the only twist is inside the stem. Take pedir (to ask for). Its stem is ped-. In the boot forms the e flips to i → pid-. Outside the boot — that is, nosotros — the stem stays ped-.

🧩 WATCH THE STEM CHANGE
Infinitive: pedir → Stem: ped- → e becomes i: pid- → pid+o = pido

So pido means “I ask for.” The ending -o is the same one you already use; the news is only that the stem changed to pid-. Here is the full set.

📝 THE FULL SET · pedir (to ask for / to order)
Subject (who)StemFull form
yo (I)pid-pido
tú (you, friendly)pid-pides
usted / él / ella (you-formal / he / she)pid-pide
nosotros / nosotras (we)ped-  (no change!)pedimos
ustedes / ellos / ellas (you-all / they)pid-piden

Look at the shape: four forms with pid-, and nosotros alone keeping ped-. Trace the changed forms with your finger and you draw a boot — the same outline you saw in quiero (e→ie) and puedo (o→ue). We use ustedes for a group all year (never the Spain-only vosotros).

📚 THE OTHER THREE E→I VERBS

The same change runs through all of this week's verbs:

  • servir (to serve) → sirvo, sirves, sirve, servimos, sirven
  • repetir (to repeat) → repito, repites, repite, repetimos, repiten
  • seguir (to follow) → sigo, sigues, sigue, seguimos, siguen

One small spelling note on seguir: the yo form is sigo — the silent u disappears before the -o ending (it is only there to keep the g sound hard in the other forms). The stem still changes e→i just like the rest.

💡 SEE IT IN REAL SENTENCES

Watch the pattern work on vocabulary you already own. Press ▶ to hear each sentence and say it back:

Yo pido agua. — I ask for water. (pedir → pid- + o)
El camarero sirve la comida. — The waiter serves the food. (servir → sirv- + e)
Nosotros repetimos las palabras. — We repeat the words. (nosotros = no change → repetimos)
¿Tú sigues las instrucciones? — Do you follow the instructions? (seguir → sigu- + es)
Ellos piden pizza. — They order pizza. (pedir → pid- + en)

Notice sentence 3: nosotros keeps the e (repetimos) while every other subject shows the i.

⚡ TRY IT · CONJUGATION DRILL
The subject is given — pick the form that matches. Watch out for nosotros, the one form that keeps the e. Instant feedback on each.
1. Yo ____ ayuda. (pedir — I ask for help.)
2. Nosotros ____ el postre. (servir — We serve the dessert.)
3. El profesor ____ la pregunta. (repetir — The teacher repeats the question.)
4. ¿Tú ____ a tu hermano? (seguir — Do you follow your brother?)
5. Ellas ____ tacos en el restaurante. (pedir — They order tacos.)

🔑 TODAY'S TAKEAWAY

An e→i boot verb changes its stem vowel e to i in every present form except nosotros. The endings never change — only the stem. That is the exact same boot shape you already know from e→ie and o→ue.
