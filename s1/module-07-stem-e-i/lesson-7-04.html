LESSON 7.04 · VERBS IN ACTION
A meal out, told with your new verbs
See pedir, servir, repetir and seguir at work in a real little scene — then find where these words hide in English.
📖 READ & LISTEN · EN EL RESTAURANTE

Read each sentence, then press ▶ to hear it. Every verb below is a present-tense form you now know — watch the boot verbs change (and watch nosotros stay put).

Mi familia y yo comemos en un restaurante mexicano. — My family and I eat at a Mexican restaurant.
El camarero repite el menú del día. — The waiter repeats the menu of the day. (repetir, e→i)
Yo pido tacos y mi hermana pide una ensalada. — I order tacos and my sister orders a salad. (pedir, e→i)
El camarero sirve la comida rápido. — The waiter serves the food quickly. (servir, e→i)
Nosotros seguimos la conversación y comemos juntos. — We continue the conversation and eat together. (seguir — nosotros = no change)
¿Y tú? ¿Qué pides en un restaurante? — And you? What do you order at a restaurant?
🌎 SPANISH IN YOUR WORLD · VERBS HIDING IN ENGLISH

Two of this week's verbs are cognates — you can almost read them because Spanish and English both borrowed them from the same Latin roots:

  • repetir cognate → English repeat (and repetition)
  • servir cognate → English serve (and service, servant)

When you meet a new Spanish verb, ask: does it sound like an English word I already know? Cognates like these turn a “new” word into one you half-knew all along.
