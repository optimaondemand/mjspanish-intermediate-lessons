LESSON 6.02 · THE PATTERN
The o becomes ue — everywhere but nosotros
Same boot shape as last week. Only the vowel is new. Go slowly and watch the stem.
👋 THE ONE IDEA
In these verbs the stem vowel o changes to ue in every present-tense form except nosotros. The endings stay perfectly regular — only the stem changes.

A boot verb is a normal verb with one twist: when the stem vowel is stressed, it breaks into a diphthong. Last week that was e→ie; this week it is o→ue. The nosotros form is the odd one out — its stress falls on the ending, not the stem, so the vowel stays o. Picture a boot shape around the forms that change, with nosotros sticking out the top.

🧩 HOW ONE FORM IS BUILT
1. Infinitive: poder (to be able / can) → 2. Change the stem o→ue: pued- → 3. Add the regular ending: pued+o = puedo

So puedo means “I can / I am able.” The ending -o is exactly the regular -er ending you already know — the only new move is flipping the stem o→ue first.

📝 THE FULL BOOT · poder (to be able / can)

Read down the column: puedo, puedes, puede, then the boot opens for podemos (still o!), then closes again for pueden. The highlighted nosotros row is the form outside the boot — the one that keeps the original vowel.

🔄 TWO MORE TO SEE THE RANGE

The same boot works on -ir verbs like dormir (to sleep). And one special verb, jugar (to play), does the same thing with a u instead of an o: u→ue. It is the only common u→ue verb — treat it as an honorary member of this boot.

💡 SEE IT IN REAL SENTENCES

Watch the boot work in full sentences. Press ▶ to hear each one and say it back:

Yo puedo ayudar hoy. — I can help today. (poder → pued- + o)
Tú duermes ocho horas. — You sleep eight hours. (dormir → duerm- + es)
Ella juega al fútbol. — She plays soccer. (jugar → jueg- + a, u→ue)
Nosotros volvemos a casa. — We return home. (volver — nosotros keeps the o!)
Ustedes almuerzan a la una. — You all eat lunch at one. (almorzar → almuerz- + an)

Same move every time: flip the stem o→ue (or u→ue for jugar), then add the ordinary ending — except in nosotros, where nothing changes.

⚡ TRY IT · CONJUGATION DRILL
Pick the correct form. The subject is given, so choose the one that fits — and watch out for the nosotros traps. You'll get instant feedback on each one.
1. Yo ____ estudiar ahora. (poder — I can study now.)
2. Ustedes ____ mucho los sábados. (dormir — You all sleep a lot on Saturdays.)
3. Tú ____ al básquetbol. (jugar — You play basketball.)
4. Nosotros ____ a casa tarde. (volver — We come home late.)
5. Ella ____ en la cafetería. (almorzar — She eats lunch in the cafeteria.)

🔑 TODAY'S TAKEAWAY

Flip the stem vowel o→ue (or u→ue for jugar) in every present-tense form except nosotros, where the vowel stays put. The endings never change — they are the same regular -ar / -er / -ir endings you already know. That is the whole rule.
