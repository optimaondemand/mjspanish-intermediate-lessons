LESSON 6.01 · A NEW BOOT, A FAMILIAR SHAPE
You already conquered one boot — here comes its twin
Nothing new to memorize today — just retrieve last week's boot verbs and see where this week is headed.
👋 TODAY'S BIG IDEA
Last week the stem vowel changed e→ie. This week it changes o→ue — same boot, different vowel.

In Module 5 you met your first “boot” verbs — verbs whose stem vowel changes in every present-tense form except nosotros. This week you meet a second family of boot verbs: this time the stem o becomes ue (and one lone verb, jugar, changes u→ue). The pattern is identical — only the vowel is different — so today is a gentle warm-up before the new grammar tomorrow.

🔄 FIRST · RETRIEVE LAST WEEK'S BOOT

You are not starting cold. Read these e→ie forms from Module 5 out loud — you already own them:

quiero — I want piensas — you think prefiere — he/she prefers empezamos — we begin

Notice one thing before we go on: empezamos keeps its e — the nosotros form did not change. That gap is the whole reason we call these boot verbs, and it works exactly the same for this week's o→ue verbs.
