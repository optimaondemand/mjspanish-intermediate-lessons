LESSON 6.05 · PULLING IT TOGETHER
One rule, seven verbs, a whole week
Lock in the o→ue boot, run a quick self-check, then take the short module quiz.
🔑 THE WHOLE WEEK IN ONE RULE

Flip the stem vowel o→ue (or u→ue for jugar) in every present-tense form except nosotros. The endings never change.

Your seven verbs: poder (can), dormir (sleep), volver (return), almorzar (eat lunch), encontrar (find), costar (cost), and the special u→ue verb jugar (play).

⚡ SELF-CHECK · ARE YOU READY FOR THE QUIZ?
Four to try before the module check. Pick the correct present-tense form — you'll get instant feedback on each.
1. Yo ____ ocho horas cada noche. (dormir — I sleep eight hours every night.)
2. Nosotros ____ al fútbol los domingos. (jugar — We play soccer on Sundays.)
3. Tú ____ a tus amigos en el parque. (encontrar — You meet your friends in the park.)
4. El libro ____ diez dólares. (costar — The book costs ten dollars.)
