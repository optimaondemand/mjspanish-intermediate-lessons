LESSON 12.05 · BRING IT TOGETHER
You can now “know” two ways in Spanish
One quick review, one self-check, and you're ready for the module quiz.
📋 THE WHOLE WEEK, IN ONE PLACE
  • saber = facts, information, and how to do something. Sé nadar. Yo form: sé.
  • conocer = being familiar / acquainted with a person, place, or thing. Conozco la ciudad. Yo form: conozco.
  • Personal a = goes before a specific person who receives the action. Conozco a Ana. No a before a place or thing.
  • The test: ask “fact/skill, or familiar-with a person/place?” — then, “is the object a person?” for the a.
📝 THE TWO FORMS WORTH DOUBLE-CHECKING

Only the yo forms are irregular — and they're the ones the quiz loves to test:

Verbyo (I) formEverything else
saber (facts / how-to)sésabes · sabe · sabemos · saben
conocer (familiar with)conozcoconoces · conoce · conocemos · conocen

Remember the accent on sé — it's what separates “I know” from other little words.

✅ SELF-CHECK · PUT IT ALL TOGETHER
Five mixed questions — verb choice and the personal a. Get instant feedback, then reset and try again until every one is green.
1. Yo ____ tocar la guitarra. (I know how to play the guitar.)
2. Ellos ____ a mi vecino. (They know my neighbor.)
3. Nosotros ____ el estadio nuevo. (We know the new stadium.)
4. Conozco ____ la cantante de la banda. (I know [of] the band's singer.)
5. ¿Conoces ____ el país de tus abuelos? (Do you know your grandparents' country?)
