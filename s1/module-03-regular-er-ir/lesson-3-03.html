LESSON 3.03 · NEW WORDS, KNOWN PATTERN
Ten -ER / -IR verbs to make your own
You already own the endings from yesterday — today we just load in the words that ride on them.
👋 WHY THIS IS THE EASY DAY

Yesterday you learned the -ER and -IR engines. Today is pure vocabulary — ten common verbs that all follow those engines. Once you know a verb ends in -er or -ir, you already know how to say I ___, you ___, we ___. Each word is tagged with its family so you know which "we" ending to use: -ER takes -emos, -IR takes -imos.

A few are near-cognates — they look like their English meanings. Those are marked cognate so you can lean on words you half-know already.

🔊 LISTEN & REPEAT · FIVE -ER VERBS

These are infinitives (the "to ___" form). Verbs don't carry gender, so there's no color-coding here — press play and say each one aloud. All five take the -emos "we" ending.

  • comer -ERto eat
  • beber -ERto drink
  • leer -ERto read
  • aprender -ERto learn
  • comprender -ER cognate · comprehendto understand
🔊 LISTEN & REPEAT · FIVE -IR VERBS

Same idea, different family — these five take the -imos "we" ending. Everything else matches the -ER set.

  • vivir -IRto live
  • escribir -IRto write
  • recibir -IR cognate · receiveto receive
  • abrir -IRto open
  • decidir -IR cognate · decideto decide
💡 SPOT THE COGNATES

comprender → comprehend, decidir → decide, recibir → receive. When you hear a Spanish verb that sounds like an English one and means the same thing, that's a cognate — free vocabulary. Tomorrow's reading is full of them.

📝 PUT ONE TO WORK

Because these ride on yesterday's pattern, you can already build sentences with them:

  • Yo bebo agua. — I drink water. (-ER: beb- + o)
  • Tú escribes un mensaje. — You write a message. (-IR: escrib- + es)
  • Nosotros aprendemos español. — We learn Spanish. (-ER: -emos)
  • Nosotros vivimos en la ciudad. — We live in the city. (-IR: -imos)

Same three steps as always: drop the ending, add the ending for the subject — and remember the -emos / -imos fork at "we".
