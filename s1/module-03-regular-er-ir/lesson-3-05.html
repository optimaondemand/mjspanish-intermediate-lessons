LESSON 3.05 · BRING IT TOGETHER
You can now conjugate all three verb families
One last mixed check, then the module quiz. That's the entire regular present tense — done.
🔑 THE WHOLE WEEK ON ONE CARD

Same three steps as -AR: drop the ending off the infinitive, then add the ending for the subject. Here are both new families side by side — identical except the highlighted "we" row:

Subject-ER (comer)-IR (vivir)
yocomovivo
túcomesvives
usted / él / ellacomevive
nosotros / nosotrascomemosvivimos
ustedes / ellos / ellascomenviven

Say the chant until it's automatic: -o, -es, -e, -emos / -imos, -en. The only fork is at nosotros: -emos for -ER, -imos for -IR. (Five forms — we use ustedes for a group, all year long.)

📚 AND THE WORDS YOU ADDED

Five -ER verbs: comer, beber, leer, aprender, comprender. Five -IR verbs: vivir, escribir, recibir, abrir, decidir.

Plus the cognate strategy: guess look-alikes boldly — comprender → comprehend, decidir → decide, recibir → receive, describir → describe. Dozens of -ER/-IR verbs hide inside English words you already know.

★ A WORD ON PERSEVERANCE

Two weeks ago you had never conjugated a Spanish verb. Now you can take any regular verb — -AR, -ER, or -IR — and put it in any subject, because you learned patterns, not lists. That's the payoff of steady daily work. Carry the same habit into Module 4, where verbs start to bend the rules.
