LESSON 18.03 · FROM VERBS TO A REAL DAY
Five tools for talking about your life
Verbs are the engine; these five tools are how you steer them into a whole day.
👋 WHAT THIS PAGE PULLS TOGETHER
Now that verbs are sorted, here are the five tools that turn them into a real day — the exact toolkit for your capstone.

Each tool below got its own module this semester. Read the quick recap, then take the mixed self-check. This is the language you'll reach for on Friday when you narrate Un día en mi vida.

🕐 TOOL 1 · RIGHT NOW — present progressive

estar + -ando / -iendo says what's happening at this moment: Yo estoy estudiando ahora. (I'm studying right now.) Take the stem and add -ando for -AR verbs, -iendo for -ER/-IR verbs.

hablar → hablando  ·  comer → comiendo  ·  escribir → escribiendo.

➡️ TOOL 2 · PLANS — near future

ir + a + infinitive says what you're going to do: Esta noche voy a leer un libro. (Tonight I'm going to read a book.) Conjugate only ir (voy, vas, va, vamos, van); the second verb stays in the infinitive.

This is your closer on the capstone — one sentence about what you're going to do next.

🛀 TOOL 3 · YOUR ROUTINE — reflexive verbs

Reflexive verbs describe things you do to yourself — your daily routine. The pronoun goes before the verb: Yo me levanto, me ducho, me visto. Pair them with sequence words: primero (first), luego (then), después (after).

These will be the backbone of your morning and evening sentences.

🔑 TOOL 4 · DON'T REPEAT YOURSELF — object pronouns

Direct-object pronouns (lo, la, los, las, me, te, nos) replace a noun you already said, so you don't repeat it. They match in gender/number and sit before the verb: ¿El libro? — Lo leo. (The book? — I read it.) ¿La tarea? — La hago. (The homework? — I do it.)

A small touch that makes your day sound smooth instead of choppy.

🧠 TOOL 5 · TWO WAYS TO "KNOW" — saber vs conocer

saber = to know a fact or how to do something: Yo sé hablar español. (I know how to speak Spanish.) conocer = to be familiar with a person or place: Yo conozco a María. (I know María.)

When the thing you "know" is a person, add the personal a: conozco a María. Both are yo-go in the yo form: sé (irregular) and conozco.

⚡ MIXED SELF-CHECK · THE FIVE TOOLS
One row per tool, shuffled. Pick the choice that fits, and read the feedback on each. Present tense throughout.
1. ¿Qué haces ahora mismo? — ____ la tarea. ("I'm doing the homework right now.")
2. Mañana yo ____ para el examen. ("Tomorrow I'm going to study.")
3. Nosotros ____ los dientes después del desayuno. (cepillarse)
4. ¿El libro? Yo ____ leo cada noche. ("I read it every night.")
5. Yo ____ hablar español. ("I know how to speak Spanish.")
6. Yo ____ a María; es mi amiga. ("I know María.")
🔑 TODAY'S TAKEAWAY

Verbs + these five tools = a whole day you can narrate: what you're doing right now, what you're going to do, your routine, smoother sentences with object pronouns, and the people and skills you know. That's your capstone toolkit — and you own every piece.
