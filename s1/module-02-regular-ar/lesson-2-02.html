LESSON 2.02 · THE PATTERN
Drop the ending, add the endings
This is the engine of the whole year. Go slowly — today you conjugate your first real Spanish verb.
👋 THE ONE IDEA
A regular -AR verb is a stem plus a changeable ending. Change the ending, change who is doing the action.

Every regular -AR verb starts life as an infinitive — the "to ___" form, like hablar (to speak). To use it in a real sentence, you do two things: chop off the -ar, then add the ending that matches the subject. That's it. Learn the endings once and they work on every regular -AR verb.

🧩 THE THREE STEPS
1. Infinitive: hablar → 2. Drop -ar: stem = habl- → 3. Add the ending: habl+o = hablo

So hablo means "I speak." The stem habl- carries the meaning; the ending -o tells you the subject is yo (I). Now here is the full set of endings.

📝 THE FIVE ENDINGS · hablar (to speak)
Subject (who)EndingFull form
yo (I)habl·ohablo
tú (you, friendly)habl·ashablas
usted / él / ella (you-formal / he / she)habl·ahabla
nosotros / nosotras (we)habl·amoshablamos
ustedes / ellos / ellas (you-all / they)habl·anhablan

Five endings to learn: -o / -as / -a / -amos / -an. Notice we use ustedes ("you all") for a group — that is the standard form across Latin America, and the one we use all year.

💡 SEE IT IN REAL SENTENCES

Watch the same pattern work with verbs you already recognize (all cognates — you can read them). Press ▶ to hear each sentence and say it back:

Yo estudio español. — I study Spanish. (estudiar → estudi- + o)
Tú practicas el piano. — You practice the piano. (practicar → practic- + as)
Ella usa la computadora. — She uses the computer. (usar → us- + a)
Nosotros preparamos la cena. — We prepare dinner. (preparar → prepar- + amos)
Ellos estudian juntos. — They study together. (estudiar → estudi- + an)

Same three steps every time: infinitive → drop -ar → add the ending for the subject.

⚡ TRY IT · CONJUGATION DRILL
Pick the correct form. The subject is given, so choose the ending that matches. You'll get instant feedback on each one.
1. Tú ____ para el examen. (estudiar — You study for the test.)
2. Nosotros ____ español cada día. (practicar — We practice Spanish every day.)
3. Yo ____ el diccionario. (usar — I use the dictionary.)
4. Ellas ____ en casa. (cocinar — They cook at home.)
5. Usted ____ mucho. (trabajar — You [formal] work a lot.)

🔑 TODAY'S TAKEAWAY

Every regular -AR verb follows the same three steps and the same five endings: -o / -as / -a / -amos / -an. Memorize that little chant this week and you can conjugate verbs you have never even seen before.
