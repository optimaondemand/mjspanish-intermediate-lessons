🔑 THE WHOLE WEEK ON ONE CARD

Three steps, five endings. That's the entire skill:

1. Start with the infinitive (hablar).  2. Drop -ar to get the stem (habl-).  3. Add the ending for the subject.

SubjectEndinghablar
yo-ohablo
tú-ashablas
usted / él / ella-ahabla
nosotros / nosotras-amoshablamos
ustedes / ellos / ellas-anhablan

Say the chant until it's automatic: -o, -as, -a, -amos, -an. (Five forms — we use ustedes for a group, all year long.)

📚 AND THE WORDS YOU ADDED

Ten -AR verbs: hablar, estudiar, practicar, escuchar, usar, caminar, mirar, trabajar, cocinar, viajar.

Plus the cognate strategy: guess look-alikes like estudiar → study boldly, but watch the false friends (asistir = to attend, éxito = success, ropa = clothes).
