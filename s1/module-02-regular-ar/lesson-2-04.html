📖 READ · EL DÍA DE ANA

Every verb below is a regular -AR verb in the present tense. Read it once for the gist, then again slowly. Press ▶ to hear each sentence and read along.

Ana estudia español y practica cada día. — Ana studies Spanish and practices every day.
Por la mañana, ella camina a la escuela y escucha música. — In the morning, she walks to school and listens to music.
En la clase, Ana y sus amigos usan la computadora y hablan en español. — In class, Ana and her friends use the computer and speak in Spanish.
Por la tarde, ella trabaja en casa y cocina con su familia. — In the afternoon, she works at home and cooks with her family.
¡Ana practica mucho! — Ana practices a lot!
WORD HELP
cada díaevery daypor la mañana / tardein the morning / afternoon
sus amigosher friendsen casaat home

Notice how estudia, practica, camina use the -a ending (Ana = she), while usan and hablan use -an (Ana and her friends = they). The endings do the work of showing who.

🌐 SPANISH IN YOUR WORLD · COGNATE VERBS

Here's a gift English gave back and forth with Spanish: cognate verbs — verbs that look alike and mean the same thing in both languages, because both borrowed them from Latin. You can often guess them on sight:

estudiar→ study
practicar→ practice
usar→ use
preparar→ prepare

But watch out — a few Spanish words look like an English word yet mean something completely different. We call those false friends. For example, asistir looks like "assist," but it actually means to attend (to go to something). Cognates are your friends; false friends set a trap. The check below sorts the real from the fake.

⚡ TRY IT · TRUE COGNATE OR FALSE FRIEND?
Does the Spanish word really mean what its English look-alike suggests? Pick one for each. Read the options carefully — the order changes.
1. estudiar — looks like "study"
2. asistir — looks like "assist"
3. éxito — looks like "exit"
4. preparar — looks like "prepare"
5. ropa — looks like "rope"
💡 THE STRATEGY

Cognates give you free vocabulary — guess them boldly. But when a word matters, double-check the sneaky ones. A tiny number of false friends (asistir, éxito, ropa) cause most of the mix-ups, so just knowing they exist keeps you safe.
