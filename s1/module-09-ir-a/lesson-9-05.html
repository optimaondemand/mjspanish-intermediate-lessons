LESSON 9.05 · BRING IT TOGETHER
You can now talk about the future
One quick review, one self-check, then the module quiz.
🔑 THE WHOLE WEEK ON ONE CARD

Say the chant out loud once more — the five forms of ir:

yo voy tú vas usted/él/ella va nosotros vamos ustedes/ellos/ellas van

And the two ways you use it:

PatternExampleMeans
ir + a + placeVoy al parque.I go to the park.
ir a + infinitiveVoy a estudiar.I'm going to study. (near future)

Two reminders: a + el = al (but a la stays two words); and the near future is still built on present-tense ir — no past tense here.

⚡ SELF-CHECK · MIX IT ALL TOGETHER
Four quick ones from across the week. Instant feedback on each — then you're ready for the quiz.
1. Nosotros ____ a la plaza. (We go to the plaza.)
2. Ella ____ a leer un libro. (She's going to read a book.)
3. How do you say "I go to the gym"? (el gimnasio)
4. What does la biblioteca mean?
✅ NOW: THE MODULE 9 CHECK

You're ready. Head to the Module 9 Check in Canvas — a short auto-graded quiz on the verb ir, the near future, the al contraction, and this week's places.

  • Unlimited attempts — take it as many times as you need.
  • You need 70% to unlock Module 10.
  • You'll see the correct answer and a short "why" after each attempt — use it to learn.

Tip: if a verb form trips you up, come back and say the voy / vas / va / vamos / van chant once more before you retry.

Learning to talk about the future takes a small step every day — a chant here, a sentence there. If the quiz doesn't go perfectly the first time, that's not failure; that's practice. Come back, try again, and watch it click. Steady beats fast.
