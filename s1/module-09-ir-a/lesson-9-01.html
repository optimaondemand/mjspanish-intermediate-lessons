LESSON 9.01 · A NEW SUPERPOWER: TALKING ABOUT THE FUTURE
You can already say "right now." This week: "what's next."
Nothing to memorize today — just see what you own, and get a map of the week.
👋 TODAY'S BIG IDEA
Last week you learned to say what you are doing right now. This week you learn to say where you go — and what you are going to do next.

In Module 8 you built the present progressive — estoy estudiando ("I am studying right now"). This week we add one small, powerful verb: ir (to go). With it you can say where you're headed (voy al parque) and — the big prize — what you are going to do (voy a estudiar). That's how Spanish talks about the near future, and you'll own it by Friday.

Here's the friendly part: this "near future" is still built with a present-tense verb. You are not learning a whole new tense — just one new verb and one simple pattern.

🔄 FIRST · WHAT YOU ALREADY OWN

You are not starting from zero. Read these out loud — you already understand every one:

Estoy estudiando — I'm studying (right now) Yo hablo — I speak Nosotros comemos — we eat Ella practica — she practices

You already conjugate regular -AR / -ER / -IR verbs, the "boot" stem-changers, and the present progressive. This week's new verb, ir, plugs right into everything you can already do.

🎯 BY THE END OF THIS WEEK YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
conjugate ir (to go) in the present: voy, vas, va, vamos, van.
2
say where you go using ir + a + a place (voy al parque).
3
talk about the near future using ir a + an infinitive (voy a estudiar — I'm going to study).
📚 A 30-SECOND HEAD START · YOU DO THIS IN ENGLISH

English has the exact same trick. Look at how you already talk about the future:

  • "I am going to study tonight." — you use going to + an action.
  • "We are going to eat later." — same pattern.

Spanish does it the very same way: a form of ir ("to go") + a + the action. Once you see that, the whole week clicks into place.
