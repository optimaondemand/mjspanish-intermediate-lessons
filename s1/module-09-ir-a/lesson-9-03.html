LESSON 9.03 · NEW WORDS, KNOWN PATTERN
Eight places to go
You already own ir a from yesterday — today we load in the places to send it.
👋 WHY THIS IS THE EASY DAY

Yesterday you learned the ir a pattern. Today is pure vocabulary — eight common places you can drop right into it. As soon as you know a place, you can say Voy a… ("I go to…") plus that place.

These are nouns, so they carry grammatical gender. We color-code it so your eye learns the article along with the word:

el = masculine la = feminine
🔊 LISTEN & REPEAT · EIGHT PLACES

Press play and say each one aloud. Notice the color of the article — blue el or red la.

  • el parquethe park
  • el cinethe movie theater
  • la escuelathe school
  • la tiendathe store / shop
  • la plazathe plaza / town square
  • el restaurantethe restaurant
  • la bibliotecathe library
  • el gimnasiothe gym
💡 DON'T FORGET THE al

With the masculine (el) places, a + el squeezes into al: Voy al parque, Voy al cine, Voy al gimnasio. With the feminine (la) places, it stays two words: Voy a la escuela, Voy a la biblioteca.

📝 PUT ONE TO WORK

Because these ride on yesterday's ir a pattern, you can already build sentences with them:

  • Yo voy al gimnasio. — I go to the gym. (el gimnasio → al)
  • Nosotros vamos a la biblioteca. — We go to the library.
  • Ella va al cine. — She goes to the movies.

Same recipe: a form of ir → a (or al before a masculine place) → the place.
