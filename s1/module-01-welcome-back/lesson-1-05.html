LESSON 1.05 · PULL IT ALL TOGETHER
Look how much you already own
One week in and you've retrieved a whole foundation. Let's check it, then step into the verbs.
🔄 TODAY'S BIG IDEA
This week was about proving to yourself that the foundation is still there — and it is.

Module 1 didn't teach a single new verb, and that was on purpose. You spent the week reactivating what you already own so that next week's new material lands on solid ground. Let's name exactly what you've got, run one mixed self-check, and then aim you at the Module 1 quiz.

✅ WHAT YOU NOW OWN (COMING INTO MODULE 2)
  • ✓
    SER vs ESTAR — both mean “to be,” but ser tells who/what something is, and estar tells how or where it is.
  • ✓
    Grammatical gender — nouns are masculine (el hermano) or feminine (la hermana), with matching articles.
  • ✓
    GUSTAR — how to say what you like: me gusta…
  • ✓
    Subject pronouns — yo, tú, usted / él / ella, nosotros / nosotras, ustedes, ellos / ellas.
  • ✓
    The concept of conjugation — a verb's ending changes to match who is doing the action.
💭 QUICK CHECK-IN · NO WRONG ANSWER
Reading that list — how does the foundation feel right now?
⚡ MIXED SELF-CHECK · A LITTLE OF EVERYTHING
Four quick questions across the whole week. Pick one per row — instant feedback, and no penalty for trying again.
1. Ella ____ simpática. (She is friendly.)
2. Which article fits: ____ casa? (the house)
3. ____ la música. (I like music.)
4. Nosotros ____ en la escuela. (We are at school.)
📝 NOW · THE MODULE 1 CHECK

When the self-check feels comfortable, take the Module 1 Check quiz in Canvas. A few things to know so there's no pressure:

  • It is a short diagnostic review — ser vs estar, gender and articles, gustar, subject pronouns, and a couple of place-name items from yesterday.
  • Unlimited attempts. It is practice, not a trap — take it as many times as you like.
  • You need 70% to advance to Module 2. Miss something? Review and retake — that's how it's meant to work.

This quiz doesn't feed a big grade; it's there to make sure your foundation is steady before the verbs begin.
