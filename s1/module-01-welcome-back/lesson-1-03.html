LESSON 1.03 · DUST OFF WHAT YOU ALREADY KNOW
Your words are still in there — let's wake them up
Greetings, family, and describing words come back fast. Play each word, say it out loud, and let your ear remember.
👋 TODAY'S BIG IDEA
You don't relearn a language — you reactivate it. A few minutes of hearing and saying, and last year's words snap back.

Today is a warm-up, not a workout. Below are word sets you built last year: greetings, family, and describing words. Each one has a play button — press it, listen to a native speaker, then echo it back. Speaking along is the fastest way to bring a word out of storage.

💭 QUICK CHECK-IN · NO WRONG ANSWER
Before you play anything — how much Spanish vocabulary feels “still there” right now?
📣 SET 1 · GREETINGS & COURTESY

Press play, listen, and say each one back. These are your everyday openers:

  • ¡Hola!Hi! / Hello!
  • Buenos díasGood morning
  • ¿Cómo estás?How are you?
  • Muy bien, graciasVery well, thank you
  • Hasta luegoSee you later
👪 SET 2 · LA FAMILIA

Family words carry grammatical gender. All year we color them so you can see it at a glance: masculine = blue, feminine = red.

  • la madrethe mother
  • el padrethe father
  • la hermanathe sister
  • el hermanothe brother
  • la abuelathe grandmother
  • el abuelothe grandfather
🎨 SET 3 · DESCRIBING WORDS

These are the adjectives you used with ser and estar last year. Play, listen, echo:

  • alto / altatall
  • simpático / simpáticanice, friendly
  • grandebig
  • contento / contentahappy, content
  • cansado / cansadatired

Remember: adjectives that end in -o / -a shift to match who they describe — alto for a boy, alta for a girl. Words like grande stay the same for everyone.
