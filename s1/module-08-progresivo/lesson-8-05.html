LESSON 8.05 · BRINGING IT TOGETHER
You can now say what's happening right now
One formula, ten action words, one real-world link. Let's lock it in and prove it.
📚 THE WHOLE WEEK ON ONE CARD
  • The formula: estar (conjugated) + the -ando/-iendo form = “is/are ___-ing right now.”
  • estar: estoy · estás · está · estamos · están (for a group we use están).
  • The ending: -AR → -ando (hablando); -ER/-IR → -iendo (comiendo, escribiendo).
  • The spelling tweak: a vowel before the ending → -yendo: leer → leyendo.
  • Simple vs progressive: Yo estudio = a habit; Yo estoy estudiando = right now. Both are present tense.
  • In the world: Univisión and Telemundo — Spanish on US television every day.
✅ SELF-CHECK · ARE YOU READY FOR THE QUIZ?
Try these on your own. If they feel smooth, you're ready. Instant feedback on each.
1. Nosotros ____ la cena. (cocinar — We are cooking dinner.)
2. What is the -ando/-iendo form of escribir (to write)?
3. Ustedes ____ en la clase. (leer — You all are reading in class.)
🎯 YOU'RE READY IF YOU CAN…
  • ✓ build the formula out loud: estar + -ando/-iendo.
  • ✓ pick -ando for -AR verbs and -iendo for -ER/-IR verbs.
  • ✓ remember leer → leyendo.
  • ✓ tell the difference between “I study” (habit) and “I am studying” (right now).
📝 THIS MODULE'S TASK · «¿Qué estás haciendo?»

Your graded piece for Module 8 is a short caption task. In the Canvas assignment, write 3–4 sentences that each say what someone is doing right now, using the progressive. Think of it like captioning photos on your phone.

Two models to follow:

  • Yo estoy escuchando música en este momento. — I am listening to music right now.
  • Mi mamá está cocinando y mi hermano está jugando. — My mom is cooking and my brother is playing.

Graded on completion and effort — use the week's action words, and keep everything in the “right now” present. You may write from notes.

✅ NOW · TAKE THE MODULE 8 CHECK

Head to the Module 8 Check in Canvas. It's a short auto-graded quiz on building the progressive and this week's action words.

  • Unlimited attempts — retake it until it clicks.
  • You need 70% to unlock Module 9.
  • Feedback shows after each try — read it; that's where the learning is.

Then submit your ¿Qué estás haciendo? caption task above. That's Module 8 complete.
