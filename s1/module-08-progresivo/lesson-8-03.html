LESSON 8.03 · NEW WORDS, KNOWN FORMULA
Ten action words, ready for “right now”
You already own the formula from yesterday — today we load in the -ando/-iendo forms you'll use most.
👋 WHY THIS IS THE EASY DAY

Yesterday you learned the formula: estar + the -ando/-iendo form. Today is pure vocabulary — ten common action words already in their -ando/-iendo shape, so you can drop them straight into estoy…, estás…, está… The infinitive is shown too, so you can see where each one comes from.

Watch leyendo especially — that's the spelling tweak from yesterday (leer → leyendo). Listen to each one in a native voice, then test yourself with the flip cards.

🔊 LISTEN & REPEAT · TEN -ando/-iendo FORMS

Each entry shows the -ando/-iendo form with its infinitive in parentheses. Verbs don't carry gender, so there is no color-coding here — press play and say each one aloud.

  • hablandospeaking (hablar)
  • estudiandostudying (estudiar)
  • escuchandolistening (escuchar)
  • cocinandocooking (cocinar)
  • jugandoplaying (jugar)
  • comiendoeating (comer)
  • corriendorunning (correr)
  • escribiendowriting (escribir)
  • viendowatching / seeing (ver)
  • leyendoreading (leer → leyendo)
🕑 TWO LITTLE TIME WORDS

These signal “right now” — they pair naturally with the progressive.

  • ahoranow
  • ahora mismoright now
  • en este momentoat this moment
💡 ONE TO WATCH · leyendo

Nine of these ten follow the plain rule: -AR → -ando, -ER/-IR → -iendo. The tenth, leyendo, has the little spelling change from yesterday — because leer's stem ends in a vowel, the i flips to a y. Say it: le-YEN-do.

📝 PUT ONE TO WORK

Because these ride on yesterday's formula, you can already build “right now” sentences:

  • Yo estoy escuchando música ahora. — I am listening to music now.
  • Tú estás cocinando en este momento. — You are cooking at this moment.
  • Ellos están leyendo ahora mismo. — They are reading right now.

Same formula as always: estar for the subject, then the -ando/-iendo word.
