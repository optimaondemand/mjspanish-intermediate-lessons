LESSON 8.02 · THE FORMULA
estar + the “-ing” form
Two parts, one small rule for the ending. Build it slowly today and it works on every verb.
👋 THE ONE FORMULA
Present progressive = estar (conjugated) + the -ando / -iendo form of the action.

To say what someone is doing right now, you put two pieces together. Part one is estar, conjugated for the subject — that's the piece that carries who. Part two is the action verb in its special “-ing” form, called the gerundio (some teachers call it the present participle) — that piece never changes for the subject.

Yo estoy hablando. — I am speaking. estar (estoy) + the -ing form of hablar (hablando).

📝 PART ONE · estar (the part that shows WHO)

You already know these five forms — this is the same estar you use for feelings and location:

Subject (who)estar
yo (I)estoy
tú (you, friendly)estás
usted / él / ella (you-formal / he / she)está
nosotros / nosotras (we)estamos
ustedes / ellos / ellas (you-all / they)están

For a group we use ustedes (están) — the standard form across Latin America, and the one we use all year.

🧩 PART TWO · the -ando / -iendo form (the part that shows the ACTION)

Take the infinitive, drop its ending, and add one of two endings — which one depends only on whether the verb is -AR or -ER / -IR:

Verb typeAddExample
-AR verbstem + -andohablar → habl- → hablando (speaking)
-ER verbstem + -iendocomer → com- → comiendo (eating)
-IR verbstem + -iendovivir → viv- → viviendo (living)
-AR → -ando -ER / -IR → -iendo

Only two endings to remember: -ando for -AR verbs, -iendo for -ER and -IR verbs. The gerundio never changes for the subject — only estar does.

💡 ONE SPELLING TWEAK TO KNOW

When an -er/-ir stem ends in a vowel, the -iendo would put three vowels in a row — so the i becomes a y. The one you'll use most:

leer → le- + iendo → leyendo (reading) — not “leiendo.”

🔊 SEE IT IN REAL SENTENCES

Same formula every time — estar for the subject, then the -ando/-iendo form. Press ▶ to hear each sentence and say it back:

Yo estoy hablando por teléfono. — I am talking on the phone. (estoy + hablar → hablando)
Tú estás comiendo ahora. — You are eating right now. (estás + comer → comiendo)
Ella está escribiendo en su cuaderno. — She is writing in her notebook. (está + escribir → escribiendo)
Nosotros estamos estudiando español. — We are studying Spanish. (estamos + estudiar → estudiando)
Ellos están leyendo un libro. — They are reading a book. (están + leer → leyendo)

Notice: the -ando/-iendo word stays the same in every sentence — only estar changes to match the subject.

⚡ TRY IT · BUILD THE PROGRESSIVE
Pick the correct “right now” form. Match estar to the subject and use the right -ando/-iendo ending. Instant feedback on each one.
1. Yo ____ con mi amiga. (hablar — I am talking with my friend.)
2. Nosotros ____ en la cocina. (comer — We are eating in the kitchen.)
3. Ella ____ una carta. (escribir — She is writing a letter.)
4. Ellos ____ en la biblioteca. (leer — They are reading at the library.)
5. Tú ____ para el examen. (estudiar — You are studying for the test.)

🔑 TODAY'S TAKEAWAY

The formula is always the same: estar (matched to the subject) + the -ando (for -AR) or -iendo (for -ER/-IR) form. Only estar changes; the -ando/-iendo word stays put. And remember the one tweak: leer → leyendo.
